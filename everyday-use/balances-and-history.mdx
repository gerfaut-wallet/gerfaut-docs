description: "Read a wallet's balance, pending amounts and transaction history in Gerfaut, sync it by hand, hide every amount and export the history as CSV."
---

Each wallet shows its balance in the unit you chose, the amounts still waiting for a block, and its transaction history, with the time of the last sync always in view.

## Open a wallet

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Select the wallet switcher at the top of the sidebar and pick a wallet. The switcher lists every wallet of the selected network with its balance. The wallet opens on its Overview, and the sidebar pages (**Transactions**, **UTXOs**, **Policy**, **Receive**) all refer to it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Select a wallet card on the home screen. The wallet page opens with the balance on top and two tabs, `Transactions` and `UTXOs`, each with its count.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Read the balance

`Total balance` shows the balance in BTC or in sats, with the fiat value under it when you turned it on in [Units and fiat](/settings/units-and-fiat). When transactions are still outside a block, the total already counts them and an amber line under it shows what is in transit, such as `+0.00050000 BTC` or `−31 000 sats`.

A note replaces that line when the figure cannot be trusted:

- `Not synced yet.` on a wallet that never synced.
- `Sync failed: nothing fetched yet.` when the first sync failed.
- `Sync failed: showing the last known balance.` when a later sync failed.

On desktop, three links under the figure count the wallet's UTXOs, transactions and keys and open the matching page, and the Overview adds five cards around the balance:

- **Bitcoin price:** The price in your currency, with the change over `1D`, `1W`, `1M` or `1Y`.
- **Shortcuts:** `Backup & sync` and `Manage wallets`, two links into the settings.
- **Watch status:** `Last sync`, `Backend` and `Block`.
- **Balance history:** The balance of the wallet over time, as a chart.
- **Latest activity:** The five latest transactions.

## Sync a wallet

Gerfaut reads the chain when you ask it to, and on a schedule if you turn one on in [Notifications](/settings/notifications).

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Select **Sync all** at the bottom of the sidebar. It syncs every wallet of the selected network, one after the other, and reads `Syncing…` meanwhile.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Select the sync button in the app bar of the home screen or of a wallet page, or pull the home screen down. A message such as `2 wallets synced` confirms the result.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The line under the page title says where the figures come from: `Synced 2 min ago · mempool.space`, `Syncing…`, or `Sync failed` with the reason.

<Tip>
A sync only looks at the addresses Gerfaut already revealed. For coins received beyond the gap limit, open **Settings** > **Wallets** and select **Rescan** on the wallet.
</Tip>

## Browse the history

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Select **Transactions** in the sidebar. Each row reads `Received` or `Sent`, the date or the truncated transaction id while it is not mined, a status pill and the signed amount.

    <Frame caption="Every transaction of the wallet is listed with its direction, date, status and value.">
      <img src="/images/desktop/transactions.webp" alt="The Transactions page on desktop: 268 rows, each with a direction, a date, a Confirmed pill and the amount in BTC and euros." />
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Open the `Transactions` tab of the wallet page. Each row shows the direction arrow, the date, a status glyph and the signed amount.

    <Frame caption="The wallet page keeps the balance on top and the full history right under it.">
      <img src="/images/android/wallet.webp" alt="A wallet page on Android with the total balance, the Transactions and UTXOs tabs with their counts, the transaction rows and the Receive button." width="360" />
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Pending transactions come first, then the confirmed ones, newest first. The status pill reads `Pending` until the transaction is in a block, then `1 conf` to `5 conf`, then `Confirmed` from six confirmations on. Select a row to open it: see [Transaction details](/everyday-use/transaction-details).

A very active watched address loads its history in rounds. Select **Load older transactions** at the end of the list to fetch the next one.

## Hide every amount

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Select **Hide amounts** at the bottom of the sidebar. Select **Show amounts** to bring them back.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Select **More**, then **Hide balances**, on the home screen or on a wallet page. The same menu then offers **Show balances**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Every amount becomes `•••••`: balances, transaction rows, the chart, notifications and the Android widgets. Counts and policies stay visible.

## Rename a wallet

On desktop, select the wallet name at the top of the Overview, type the new name and press Enter. On Android, select the wallet name in the app bar, or **More** > **Rename wallet**, then **Save**. **Settings** > **Wallets** > **Rename** does the same on both platforms.

## Export the history as CSV

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Select **Export** in the sidebar.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    On the wallet page, select **More**, then **Export CSV**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Set the filters, then export:

- **Date range:** Leave `From` and `To` empty to export the full history.
- **Direction:** `All`, `Received` or `Sent`.
- **Include pending:** Pending transactions have no date, so they only export without date bounds.

The counter reads how many transactions the filters keep. Select **Export CSV…** on desktop, or **Save file** or **Share** on Android. The file is named after the wallet, such as `cold-storage-transactions.csv`, with one row per transaction, oldest first: `txid`, `date_utc`, `block_height`, `confirmations`, `direction`, `amount_sats`, `amount_btc` and `fee_sats`. Everything stays on the device.
