Open a wallet to read its balance, sync it and browse its transactions.

## Open a wallet

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Select the wallet switcher at the top of the sidebar, then a wallet. Overview, Transactions, UTXOs, Policy, Receive and Export then show that wallet.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Select a wallet card on the home screen. The wallet page opens with the balance on top, then the `Transactions` and `UTXOs` tabs.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Read the balance

`Total balance` already counts the transactions that are not in a block yet. An amber line under it shows the part in transit, such as `+0.00050000 BTC`. To add a fiat value under each amount, see [Units and fiat](/settings/units-and-fiat).

A note replaces the amber line when the figure is not current:

- **`Not synced yet.`:** The wallet never synced.
- **`Sync failed: nothing fetched yet.`:** The first sync failed.
- **`Sync failed: showing the last known balance.`:** A later sync failed.

<Accordion title="Read the Overview cards on desktop">
  Three links under the balance count the UTXOs and the transactions, and sum up the policy, such as `2 of 3 keys`. Each one opens the matching page. Five cards surround the balance:

  - **Bitcoin price:** The price in your currency, with its change over `1D`, `1W`, `1M` or `1Y`. With mempool.space as the price source, only `1M` and `1Y`.
  - **Shortcuts:** `Backup & sync` and `Manage wallets`, two links into the settings.
  - **Watch status:** `Last sync`, `Backend` and `Block`, plus `Live watch` when [Live cannot follow every address](/settings/notifications#see-which-wallets-wait-for-the-next-sync).
  - **Balance history:** The balance of the wallet over time, as a chart.
  - **Latest activity:** The five latest transactions.
</Accordion>

## Sync a wallet

Gerfaut syncs your wallets when it opens. With [notifications](/settings/notifications#check-when-notifications-arrive) on, it also syncs a wallet when Live hears a transaction on it, or at each background check on Android while Live is off.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Select **Sync all** at the bottom of the sidebar to sync every wallet of the network now. The line under the title of Transactions and UTXOs then reads `Synced 2 min ago · mempool.space`, or starts with `Sync failed`. Point at a failure to read its reason, or see `Last sync` on the Overview.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Pull the home screen down, or select the sync button in the app bar. The line on the wallet page then reads `Synced 2 min ago · mempool.space`, or starts with `Sync failed`, with the reason below.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
A sync also finds a payment to an address Gerfaut never showed, up to the gap limit past the last one it revealed. For coins further out, raise the `Gap limit` in **Settings** > **Wallets**, then select **Rescan** on the wallet.
</Note>

## Browse the history

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Select **Transactions** in the sidebar. Each row shows its status:

    - **`Pending`:** The transaction is not in a block yet.
    - **`1 conf` to `5 conf`:** The number of confirmations so far.
    - **`Confirmed`:** Six confirmations or more.

    <Frame caption="Every transaction of the wallet is listed with its direction, date, status and value.">
      <img src="/images/desktop/transactions.webp" alt="The Transactions page on desktop, counting 299 transactions, each row with a direction, a date, a Confirmed pill and the amount in BTC and euros." />
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Select the `Transactions` tab of the wallet page. A clock marks a pending transaction, and a check mark a transaction in a block.

    <Frame caption="The wallet page keeps the balance on top and the full history right under it.">
      <img src="/images/android/wallet.webp" alt="A wallet page on Android with the total balance, the Transactions and UTXOs tabs with their counts, the transaction rows and the Receive button." width="360" />
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Pending transactions come first, then the others, newest first. Select a row to [open the transaction](/everyday-use/transaction-details).

## Hide every amount

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Select **Hide amounts** at the bottom of the sidebar. **Show amounts** brings them back.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Select **More**, then **Hide balances**. **Show balances** brings them back.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Every amount becomes `•••••`, on balances, transactions, the desktop chart and the Android widgets. Notifications leave the amount out.

## Export the history as CSV

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the export page">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Desktop">Select **Export** in the sidebar.</Tab>
      <Tab title="Android">On the wallet page, select **More**, then **Export CSV**.</Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the filters">
    Under `Date range`, leave `From` and `To` empty to export the full history, or set the first and last day:

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Desktop">Enter each day as `YYYY-MM-DD`, such as `2026-01-31`. Gerfaut reads whole days in UTC, the time zone of the dates in the file.</Tab>
      <Tab title="Android">Select `From` or `To` and pick the day.</Tab>
    </Tabs>

    Under `Direction`, keep `All` or select `Received` or `Sent`. Pending transactions have no date, so `Include pending` only applies without dates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the file">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Desktop">Select **Export CSV…** and pick where to save the file.</Tab>
      <Tab title="Android">Select **Save file** or **Share**.</Tab>
    </Tabs>

    The file carries the wallet name, such as `cold-storage-transactions.csv`. It holds one row per transaction, oldest first, with the transaction id, date, block, confirmations, direction, amount and fee.
  </Step>
</Steps>
