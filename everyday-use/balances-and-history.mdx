description: "Open a wallet in Gerfaut, read its balance and pending amounts, sync it, browse its transactions, hide every amount and export the history as CSV."
---

Open a wallet to read its balance, sync it and browse its transactions. The time of the last sync stays in view under the page title.

## Open a wallet

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Select the wallet switcher at the top of the sidebar, then select a wallet. The switcher lists every wallet of the selected network with its balance. The wallet opens on its Overview, and **Transactions**, **UTXOs**, **Policy** and **Receive** in the sidebar all refer to it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Select a wallet card on the home screen. The wallet page opens with the balance on top and two tabs, `Transactions` and `UTXOs`, each with its count.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Read the balance

`Total balance` shows the balance in BTC or in sats, with the fiat value under it when you turned it on in [Units and fiat](/settings/units-and-fiat). The total already counts the transactions still outside a block. An amber line under it shows what is in transit, such as `+0.00050000 BTC` or `−31 000 sats`.

A note replaces that line when the figure cannot be trusted:

- **`Not synced yet.`:** The wallet never synced.
- **`Sync failed: nothing fetched yet.`:** The first sync failed.
- **`Sync failed: showing the last known balance.`:** A later sync failed.

<Accordion title="Read the Overview cards on desktop">
  Three links under the figure count the wallet's UTXOs, transactions and keys and open the matching page. Five cards surround the balance:

  - **Bitcoin price:** The price in your currency, with the change over `1D`, `1W`, `1M` or `1Y`.
  - **Shortcuts:** `Backup & sync` and `Manage wallets`, two links into the settings.
  - **Watch status:** `Last sync`, `Backend` and `Block`.
  - **Balance history:** The balance of the wallet over time, as a chart.
  - **Latest activity:** The five latest transactions.
</Accordion>

## Sync a wallet

Gerfaut reads the chain when you ask it to, and on a schedule if you turn one on in [Notifications](/settings/notifications).

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Select **Sync all** at the bottom of the sidebar. It syncs every wallet of the selected network, one after the other, and reads `Syncing…` meanwhile.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Select the sync button in the app bar of the home screen or of a wallet page, or pull the home screen down. A message such as `2 wallets synced` confirms the result.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The line under the page title says where the figures come from: `Synced 2 min ago · mempool.space`, `Syncing…`, or `Sync failed` with the reason.

<Note>
A sync only looks at the addresses Gerfaut already revealed. For coins received beyond the gap limit, open **Settings** > **Wallets** and select **Rescan** on the wallet.
</Note>

## Browse the history

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Select **Transactions** in the sidebar. Each row reads `Received` or `Sent`, the date or the truncated transaction id while it is not mined, a status pill and the signed amount.

    <Frame caption="Every transaction of the wallet is listed with its direction, date, status and value.">
      <img src="/images/desktop/transactions.webp" alt="The Transactions page on desktop: 268 rows, each with a direction, a date, a Confirmed pill and the amount in BTC and euros." />
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Select the `Transactions` tab of the wallet page. Each row shows the direction arrow, the date, a status glyph and the signed amount.

    <Frame caption="The wallet page keeps the balance on top and the full history right under it.">
      <img src="/images/android/wallet.webp" alt="A wallet page on Android with the total balance, the Transactions and UTXOs tabs with their counts, the transaction rows and the Receive button." width="360" />
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Pending transactions come first, then the confirmed ones, newest first. Select a row to open it, as described in [Open a transaction](/everyday-use/transaction-details).

- **`Pending`:** The transaction is not in a block yet.
- **`1 conf` to `5 conf`:** The number of confirmations so far.
- **`Confirmed`:** Six confirmations or more.

<Accordion title="Load the history of a very active address">
  A very active watched address loads its history in rounds. Select **Load older transactions** at the end of the list to fetch the next one.
</Accordion>

## Hide every amount

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Select **Hide amounts** at the bottom of the sidebar. Select **Show amounts** to bring them back.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Select **More**, then **Hide balances**, on the home screen or on a wallet page. The same menu then offers **Show balances**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Every amount becomes `•••••`: balances, transaction rows, the chart, notifications and the Android widgets. Counts and policies stay visible.

## Rename a wallet

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Select the wallet name at the top of the Overview, enter the new name and press Enter.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Select the wallet name in the app bar, or **More** > **Rename wallet**, then select **Save**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

**Settings** > **Wallets** > **Rename** does the same on both platforms.

## Export the history as CSV

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the export page">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Desktop">Select **Export** in the sidebar.</Tab>
      <Tab title="Android">On the wallet page, select **More**, then **Export CSV**.</Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the filters">
    The counter reads how many transactions the filters keep.

    - **Date range:** Leave `From` and `To` empty to export the full history.
    - **Direction:** `All`, `Received` or `Sent`.
    - **Include pending:** Pending transactions have no date, so they only export without date bounds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export the file">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Desktop">Select **Export CSV…** and pick where to save the file.</Tab>
      <Tab title="Android">Select **Save file** or **Share**.</Tab>
    </Tabs>

    The file is named after the wallet, such as `cold-storage-transactions.csv`, and stays on the device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The file holds one row per transaction, oldest first, with these columns: `txid`, `date_utc`, `block_height`, `confirmations`, `direction`, `amount_sats`, `amount_btc` and `fee_sats`.
