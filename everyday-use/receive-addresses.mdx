Get the next unused receive address of a wallet, with its QR code, and audit the addresses the wallet already revealed.

<Warning>
Verify the address on your signing device before you share it. A compromised computer or phone could show you an address that is not yours.
</Warning>

## Get an address

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Receive page">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Desktop">Select **Receive** in the sidebar.</Tab>
      <Tab title="Android">Select **Receive** at the bottom of the wallet page.</Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the address">
    The card reads `Next unused address · index N`, the address and its `Derivation path`, such as `m/84'/0'/0'/0/5`. Select the QR code to enlarge it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Copy address">
    The button reads `Copied` for a moment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Skip to another address

Select **Next address** to skip ahead one unused address at a time, and **First unused** to come back. Skipping marks nothing as used. An address counts as used once a transaction on the chain pays it.

<Note>
Past the gap limit, other wallet software may not see coins sent to that address. The gap limit is 20 by default. Change it in **Settings** > **Wallets**.
</Note>

## Audit the revealed addresses

Two cards follow: `External` for receive addresses and `Change` for the change of outgoing transactions. Each row shows the index, the address, a `Used` or `Fresh` pill and the balance on it. Select **Show all** to see more than five rows, up to the first 200 addresses.
