Open a transaction to read how much moved, when, from where to where, and then the technical facts.

## Open a transaction

Select a row on the `Transactions` page or tab, or under `Latest activity` on the desktop Overview.

## Read the summary

The top panel answers how much and when:

- **Direction:** `Received`, `Sent`, `Sent to yourself` when every input and output belongs to the wallet, or `Block reward`.
- **Amount:** The net amount, with the fiat value under it when fiat is on.
- **Status:** `Pending`, the number of confirmations, or `Confirmed`, followed by the block height once mined.
- **Transaction id:** A chip under the panel. Select it to copy it.
- **Date and time:** Under the id, or `not yet mined`.

## Follow the coins on the diagram

The diagram draws the inputs on the left, a `TX` node in the middle, the outputs on the right and the fee on a branch of its own below. Each box carries an icon for its role:

- **Wallet icon:** An input spent from this wallet.
- **Return arrow:** Change that comes back to this wallet.
- **Incoming arrow:** An output received by this wallet.
- **Outgoing arrow:** An input from elsewhere, or an output that leaves.
- **Pickaxe:** Newly minted coins, in a coinbase transaction.
- **Scroll:** An `OP_RETURN` output carrying data instead of coins.

The full `Inputs` and `Outputs` lists follow the diagram, each with its count and total, an address chip to copy, and the same role icons.

<Accordion title="Read a transaction with many inputs or outputs">
  Above ten boxes on one side (five on Android), the rest fold into one box that reads `+N more inputs` or `+N more outputs`. The full lists under the diagram still show every one of them.
</Accordion>

## Check the technical facts

The `Technical` card lists `Confirmations`, `Block`, `Size`, `Virtual size`, `Weight`, `Version`, `Locktime`, `Sigops`, `Fee`, `Fee rate` in sat/vB, and `Flags`. Each flag is a chip with a tooltip:

- **`RBF`:** The sender can still bump the fee (BIP-125). `Final` means no input signals it.
- **`SegWit` and `Taproot`:** What the inputs spend.
- **`Locktime`:** The earliest block or time the transaction could be mined in.
- **`Coinbase`:** Newly minted coins, with the mining pool when known.
- **`OP_RETURN`:** The transaction carries data in an output.

Select `Raw transaction` at the bottom to show the transaction bytes, with a copy button.

## Look it up on mempool.space

<Warning>
The operator of mempool.space can link the transaction to your IP address. Go through a VPN or [Tor](/settings/tor) if that link matters to you.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select View on mempool.space">
    The button sits in the summary panel. It is absent on regtest, which has no public explorer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Open explorer">
    Gerfaut first asks you to confirm. Tick `Do not show this warning again` to skip the question next time. The transaction opens in your browser.
  </Step>
</Steps>
