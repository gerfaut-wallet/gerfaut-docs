Gerfaut is a watch-only bitcoin wallet for Windows, macOS, Linux and Android. Give it a descriptor, an extended public key or an address, and it shows the balance, the history and the coins of that wallet.

## Watch your wallets

With a watched wallet, you can:

- **Read the balance:** Open the [balance and history](/everyday-use/balances-and-history) and the [UTXOs](/everyday-use/utxos) behind it, with the fiat value beside each amount if you turn it on.
- **Open a transaction:** See it as a diagram, inputs on the left, outputs on the right, the fee on its own branch. See [Open a transaction](/everyday-use/transaction-details).
- **Receive:** Get the next receive addresses with their QR code, the used ones marked. See [Receive](/everyday-use/receive-addresses).
- **Read a policy:** Read the spending paths of a Miniscript descriptor, with each timelock counted down. See [Read a policy](/wallets/policy-and-timelocks).
- **Broadcast:** Send a transaction that your signing device signed elsewhere, after Gerfaut reads it back in plain words. See [Broadcast](/everyday-use/broadcast-a-transaction).
- **Pick the backend:** Use the public servers set by default, your own node over Esplora or Electrum, or a `.onion` address over the [Tor](/settings/tor) client Gerfaut carries. See [Backend](/settings/backends-and-your-own-node).
- **Keep it yours:** Your wallet list stays encrypted on the device, behind an optional [app lock](/settings/app-lock). Move it to another device with an encrypted [backup](/settings/backup-and-sync) file or an animated QR code.

<Tip>
Gerfaut contains no code that generates keys, handles seeds or signs transactions, and it has no Send button. Anything you paste that contains a private key or a seed phrase is refused and never stored.
</Tip>

## Get alerts with Gerfaut Premium

The apps are free and complete on their own. [Gerfaut Premium](/premium/overview) adds a server that watches the wallets you choose around the clock and alerts you within seconds when coins move, over ntfy, Telegram, e-mail or a webhook. It runs on a Premium licence key that you buy in bitcoin, without an account.
