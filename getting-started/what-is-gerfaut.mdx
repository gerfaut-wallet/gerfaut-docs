Gerfaut is a watch-only bitcoin wallet for Windows, macOS, Linux and Android. Give it a descriptor, an extended public key or an address, and it shows the balance, the history and the coins of that wallet.

## Watch your wallets

- **Read the balance:** See the [balance and history](/everyday-use/balances-and-history) and the [UTXOs](/everyday-use/utxos) behind it.
- **Open a transaction:** Follow its inputs, outputs and fee on one diagram. See [Open a transaction](/everyday-use/transaction-details).
- **Receive:** Hand out the next unused address with its QR code. See [Receive](/everyday-use/receive-addresses).
- **Read a policy:** See every spending path of a Miniscript descriptor, with its timelock counted down. See [Read a policy](/wallets/policy-and-timelocks).
- **Broadcast:** Send a transaction your signing device signed, after Gerfaut reads it back in plain words. See [Broadcast](/everyday-use/broadcast-a-transaction).
- **Get notified:** Get a notification when a transaction reaches the mempool, and again when it confirms. See [Notifications](/settings/notifications).
- **Pick the backend:** Stay on the public servers or point Gerfaut at your own node, `.onion` included. See [Backend](/settings/backends-and-your-own-node).
- **Keep it yours:** Lock Gerfaut with an [app lock](/settings/app-lock) and move your wallets with an encrypted [backup](/settings/backup-and-sync).

<Tip>
Gerfaut contains no code that generates private keys, handles seeds or signs transactions. It refuses anything that contains a private key or a seed phrase.
</Tip>

## Get alerts with Gerfaut Premium

[Gerfaut Premium](/premium/overview) adds a server that watches the wallets you pick around the clock, even while your devices are off. It alerts you over ntfy, Telegram, e-mail or a webhook, and you pay for it in bitcoin with no account.
