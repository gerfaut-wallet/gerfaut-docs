Gerfaut is written and maintained by Loïc Morel. Questions, bug reports and security reports each have their channel.

## Ask a question

Write to [info@pandul.fr](mailto:info@pandul.fr), in English or in French.

## Report a bug or suggest a change

Open an issue on the repository of the app concerned. Everything public happens in English: issues, pull requests and code.

- **Windows, macOS and Linux:** [gerfaut-wallet/gerfaut-desktop](https://github.com/gerfaut-wallet/gerfaut-desktop/issues).
- **Android:** [gerfaut-wallet/gerfaut-mobile](https://github.com/gerfaut-wallet/gerfaut-mobile/issues).
- **These pages:** [gerfaut-wallet/gerfaut-docs](https://github.com/gerfaut-wallet/gerfaut-docs/issues).

## Report a security vulnerability

<Note>
Do not report a vulnerability through a public issue. Each repository's `SECURITY.md` states the scope and the coordinated disclosure terms in full.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write to info@pandul.fr">
    Send your report to [info@pandul.fr](mailto:info@pandul.fr) and include what you can:

    - **Type:** The kind of issue and the component affected.
    - **Reproduction:** Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept.
    - **Impact:** As you assess it.
    - **Fix:** Any suggested fix.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the acknowledgement">
    You get an acknowledgement within seven days, then a triage verdict and status updates while a fix is in progress.
  </Step>
</Steps>
