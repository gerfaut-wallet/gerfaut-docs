Gerfaut is written and maintained by Loïc Morel. Questions, bug reports and security reports each have their channel.

## Ask a question

Write to [info@pandul.fr](mailto:info@pandul.fr), in English or in French.

## Report a bug or suggest a change

Open an issue on the repository of the app concerned:

- [gerfaut-wallet/gerfaut-desktop](https://github.com/gerfaut-wallet/gerfaut-desktop/issues) for Windows, macOS and Linux.
- [gerfaut-wallet/gerfaut-mobile](https://github.com/gerfaut-wallet/gerfaut-mobile/issues) for Android.
- [gerfaut-wallet/gerfaut-docs](https://github.com/gerfaut-wallet/gerfaut-docs/issues) for these pages.

Everything public happens in English: issues, pull requests and code.

## Report a security vulnerability

Do not report a vulnerability through a public issue. Write to [info@pandul.fr](mailto:info@pandul.fr) and include what you can: the type of issue and the component affected, steps to reproduce or a proof of concept, the impact as you assess it, and any suggested fix. You get an acknowledgement within 7 days, then a triage verdict and status updates while a fix is in progress. Each repository's `SECURITY.md` states the scope and the coordinated disclosure terms in full.
