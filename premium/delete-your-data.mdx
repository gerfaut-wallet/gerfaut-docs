---
title: "Delete your data"
description: "Remove a wallet or a channel from the Gerfaut Premium server, delete the whole account with its wallets, channels and log, or let the server delete it."
---

Remove a wallet, a channel or the whole account from the Premium server, in **Settings** > **Premium** on a device with full access. Gerfaut asks you to [confirm with your app lock](/premium/devices#confirm-sensitive-actions) before it unwatches a wallet, removes a channel or deletes the account.

## Remove a wallet from the server

Select the switch of the wallet under `Watched wallets`, then **Unwatch**. The server forgets the wallet at once: its descriptor or address, what it saw on it and the alerts it sent about it. [Removing the wallet](/wallets/add-a-wallet#manage-your-wallets) from Gerfaut does the same.

A wallet refused for its coins leaves one trace for an hour, a [keyed fingerprint](/premium/overview#know-what-the-server-keeps) that keeps it from being scanned again.

## Remove a channel

Under `Channels`, select the menu of the channel line, then **Remove**. The server drops the ntfy topic, the Telegram link or the e-mail address with it.

## Delete the account

<Note>
Deleting the account stops the Premium licence key on every device, and its remaining paid time is lost.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Forget this key">
    The button sits in the `Licence` card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tick Also delete everything on the server">
    The note lists what goes: the account, the wallets it watches, the channels and the log.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Desktop">Select **Delete the account**.</Tab>
      <Tab title="Android">Select **Delete and forget**.</Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it's you">
    Confirm with your app lock, as in [Confirm sensitive actions](/premium/devices#confirm-sensitive-actions). Gerfaut forgets the licence key on this device once the server confirms the deletion.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Accordion title="Forget the licence key on this device only">
  Select **Forget this key** and confirm without ticking the box, or **Remove key from this device** on a device that waits for approval. The server disconnects the device and keeps watching your wallets. Entered again, the licence key connects the device as a new one, which waits ten days unless another device [approves it](/premium/devices#approve-a-new-device).
</Accordion>

## Let the server delete on its own

- **After the paid time:** The server deletes the account and everything it owns 90 days after the paid time ends.
- **Never paid for:** The server deletes the account 30 days after its creation.
