---

Buy a Premium licence key, save it, then enter it in Gerfaut. The licence key is the only proof of your purchase, and Gerfaut asks for nothing else: no name, no password, no account.

<Note>
Save the licence key in your password manager, or write it down, before you pay. The server keeps only a hash of it, so nobody can recover or reissue it. A lost licence key loses its paid time.
</Note>

## Buy a licence key

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Premium page">
    Go to [gerfaut-wallet.com/premium](https://gerfaut-wallet.com/premium), or select **Get Premium** in **Settings** > **Premium**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    Select `1 month` (31 days), `6 months` (183 days) or `1 year` (366 days). The page shows the price in euro. You pay the equivalent in bitcoin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Get Premium and save the licence key">
    The page shows it under `Your Premium key`. Select **Copy**, save it, tick `I wrote my key down`, then select **I saved my key, continue to payment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay in bitcoin">
    Pay over Ark, Lightning or on-chain within the hour the checkout stays open. The service starts as soon as the payment confirms.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Prepaid time runs out on its own, so there is nothing to cancel. By paying, you waive your right of withdrawal. The payment is final, except in the cases the [Terms of Sale](https://gerfaut-wallet.com/terms) list.

## Activate the licence key

Activate the licence key as soon as you have paid. The first device on the account gets full access at once, and every later one waits for its approval.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Premium">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Desktop">Select **Settings** in the sidebar, then **Premium**.</Tab>
      <Tab title="Android">Select **More** > **Settings** > **Premium**.</Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the licence key">
    Paste or type it in `Account key`. Dashes and case do not matter. A licence key never contains `l`, `o`, `0` or `1`, so check yours against the one you saved if it seems to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Activate">
    The `Licence` card reads `Active until` with the date. Gerfaut checks the licence offline from then on. A later device shows `Waiting for approval` until you [approve it](/premium/devices#approve-a-new-device).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Protect your Premium account

Once the licence key is active, the `Protect your Premium account` card in **Settings** > **Premium** lists three steps. Do them right away.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect a second device">
    Enter the licence key in Gerfaut on your computer or another phone, then [approve it](/premium/devices#approve-a-new-device) from this device. If one device is lost, the other keeps full access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the app lock">
    Select **Set up** and [turn on the app lock](/settings/app-lock#turn-on-the-app-lock). Without it, anyone holding your device unlocked could approve their own device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the licence key in a password manager">
    Select **Copy key**, paste it in your password manager, then select **Mark as done**. Nobody can send you the licence key again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The card disappears once the three steps are done. Select **Hide** to close it sooner.

Continue with [Watch a wallet](/premium/watch-a-wallet).

## Add time to a licence key

Paid time adds to the end of the time you have, so add it whenever you like.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Renew">
    In **Settings** > **Premium**, select **Renew**. Gerfaut copies the licence key to your clipboard and opens the renewal form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the licence key and pick the time">
    Paste it under `Premium key`, pick the plan under `Time to add`, then select **Add time**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    The checkout opens at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Accordion title="Renew an expired licence key">
  The `Licence` card reads `Expired on` with the date, and alerts stop seven days after it. Watching and alerting resume when you add time.
</Accordion>
