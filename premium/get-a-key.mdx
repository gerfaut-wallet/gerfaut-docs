title: "Get a key"
description: "Buy a Gerfaut Premium key on gerfaut-wallet.com, save it before you pay, activate it in the app, and add time to it when it runs out."
---

A Premium key is 16 letters and digits, shown once at purchase. It is the only proof of your purchase and the only thing the app needs: no name, no password, no account.

<Warning>
Write the key down or put it in your password manager before you pay. The server keeps a hash of the key, never the key itself, so nobody can recover, reset or re-issue it. When the key is gone, the paid time is gone with it.
</Warning>

## Buy a key

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Premium page">
    Go to [gerfaut-wallet.com/premium](https://gerfaut-wallet.com/premium), or select **Get Premium** in **Settings** > **Premium** in the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    Under `Plan`, select `1 month` (31 days, 5 €), `6 months` (183 days, 24 €) or `1 year` (366 days, 36 €). Prices are in euro; you pay in bitcoin at the rate the checkout page shows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Get Premium and save your key">
    The page shows your key under `Your account key`. Select **Copy** or write it down, tick `I wrote my key down`, then select **I saved my key, continue to payment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay in bitcoin">
    The checkout page opens in the same tab and stays open for one hour. Pay over Ark, Lightning or on-chain. The service starts the moment the payment is confirmed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
There is nothing to cancel: prepaid time runs out on its own. By paying you waive your right of withdrawal, and the payment is not refunded, as the Terms of Sale linked on the page say in full.
</Note>

## Activate the key in the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Premium">
    On desktop, select **Settings** in the sidebar, then **Premium**. On Android, select **More** > **Settings** > **Premium**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the key">
    Type or paste it in `Account key`. Dashes and case do not matter: Gerfaut formats it as `xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx`. A key never contains `l`, `o`, `0` or `1`; if yours seems to, compare it with the one you saved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Activate">
    The `Licence` card then reads `Active until` followed by the date. Gerfaut verifies the licence offline from then on, so the card shows with no network.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add time to a key

Paid time adds to the end of the time you already have, so you can add it early.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Renew in the app, or open the renewal page">
    In **Settings** > **Premium**, select **Renew**: Gerfaut copies the key to your clipboard and opens the renewal page. You can also go straight to [gerfaut-wallet.com/premium#renew](https://gerfaut-wallet.com/premium#renew).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the key and pick the time to add">
    Enter the key under `Account key`, select `1 month`, `6 months` or `1 year`, then select **Add time**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    The checkout opens right away. Your key is not shown again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An expired key reads `Expired on` with the date in the `Licence` card, and alerts stop 7 days after expiry. Reading the app's Premium section keeps working; watching and alerting resume when you add time.
