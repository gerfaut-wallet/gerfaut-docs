---
title: "Get a licence"
description: "Buy a Premium licence key on gerfaut-wallet.com, save it before you pay, activate it in Gerfaut, and add time to it before or after it runs out."
---

A Premium licence key is 16 letters and digits, shown once when you buy it. It is the only proof of your purchase and the only thing Gerfaut asks for, with no name, no password and no account.

<Note>
Write the licence key down or put it in your password manager before you pay. The server keeps a hash of it, never the licence key itself, so nobody can recover, reset or re-issue it. When the licence key is gone, the paid time is gone with it.
</Note>

## Buy a licence key

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Premium page">
    Go to [gerfaut-wallet.com/premium](https://gerfaut-wallet.com/premium), or select **Get Premium** in **Settings** > **Premium**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    Under `Plan`, select `1 month` (31 days, 5 €), `6 months` (183 days, 24 €) or `1 year` (366 days, 36 €). Prices are in euro. You pay in bitcoin at the rate the checkout page shows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Get Premium and save the licence key">
    The page shows the licence key under `Your account key`. Select **Copy** or write it down, tick `I wrote my key down`, then select **I saved my key, continue to payment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay in bitcoin">
    The checkout page opens in the same tab and stays open for one hour. Pay over Ark, Lightning or on-chain. The service starts as soon as the payment confirms.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
There is nothing to cancel, as prepaid time runs out on its own. By paying you waive your right of withdrawal, and the payment is not refunded, as the Terms of Sale linked on the page say in full.
</Note>

## Activate the licence key

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Premium">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Desktop">Select **Settings** in the sidebar, then **Premium**.</Tab>
      <Tab title="Android">Select **More** > **Settings** > **Premium**.</Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the licence key">
    Type or paste it in `Account key`. Dashes and case do not matter, as Gerfaut formats it as `xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx`. A licence key never contains `l`, `o`, `0` or `1`, so compare yours with the one you saved if it seems to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Activate">
    The `Licence` card reads `Active until` followed by the date. Gerfaut verifies the licence offline from then on, so the card shows without a network.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add time to a licence key

Paid time adds to the end of the time you already have, so add it whenever you like.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Renew">
    In **Settings** > **Premium**, select **Renew**. Gerfaut copies the licence key to your clipboard and opens the renewal page. From a browser, go straight to [gerfaut-wallet.com/premium#renew](https://gerfaut-wallet.com/premium#renew).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the licence key and pick a plan">
    Paste it under `Account key`, select `1 month`, `6 months` or `1 year`, then select **Add time**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    The checkout opens right away. The licence key is not shown again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Accordion title="Read an expired licence key">
  The `Licence` card reads `Expired on` with the date, and alerts stop seven days after expiry. The Premium section stays readable. Watching and alerting resume when you add time.
</Accordion>
