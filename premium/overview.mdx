Gerfaut Premium adds a server that watches the wallets you pick around the clock, from its own bitcoin node, and alerts you within seconds when coins move. You buy a Premium licence key, enter it in Gerfaut and switch a wallet on, with no account to open.

## Get an alert when coins move

The Premium server alerts you on these events:

- **Coins move:** A transaction spending from the wallet reaches the node's mempool, and a second alert follows when it confirms. Coins arriving are announced the same way.
- **The daily rescan finds something:** Once a day the server rescans the UTXO set and reports coins gone without the spend being seen live.
- **A timelock opens:** For a wallet with a recovery path behind a timelock, an alert comes 30 days, seven days and one day before the path becomes spendable. Another comes the day it opens.
- **The watch starts:** The first scan of a wallet you switched on is done.

## Pick where alerts go

Alerts reach the ntfy app, Telegram, an e-mail address or a webhook you run. Add them in [Channels](/premium/channels).

- **ntfy, Telegram, e-mail:** The alert names the wallet and says what happened. It carries no address, no amount and no transaction id.
- **Webhook:** The server posts to your own machine, so the delivery carries the whole event.
- **In Gerfaut:** The `Recent alerts` card lists the last 20 alerts. A red banner on the home screen tells you when the server itself stops answering.

## Know what the server keeps

Watch-only stays watch-only: the server holds no private key, sees coins move and cannot move them. It keeps these things under your licence key:

- **Wallets:** The descriptors you register and the names you gave them.
- **Addresses:** Every address derived from those descriptors, with the coins and movements seen on them.
- **Channels:** An ntfy topic, a Telegram link, an e-mail address or a webhook URL, plus the name Telegram gives a chat you link.
- **Licence key:** A hash of it. The licence key itself is never stored.

The server never keeps your e-mail unless you add one as a channel, your IP address or a request log. Stop watching a wallet, or remove it from Gerfaut, and its data leaves the server with it: see [Delete your data](/premium/delete-your-data).

<Tip>
Gerfaut talks to `api.gerfaut-wallet.com` over HTTPS. When your backend is a `.onion` address, those calls go through [Tor](/settings/tor) too.
</Tip>

## Pay for time, not for an account

One plan covers every service. You prepay one month for 5 €, six months for 24 € or one year for 36 €, in bitcoin over Ark, Lightning or on-chain. Paid time adds to the end of what you already have, and one licence key watches up to 21 wallets.

After the paid time ends, alerts continue for seven days, then stop until you add time.
