Gerfaut Premium adds a server that watches the wallets you pick around the clock, from its own bitcoin node, even while Gerfaut is closed. You buy a Premium licence key, enter it in Gerfaut and switch a wallet on, with no account to open.

## Get an alert when coins move

The Premium server alerts you when:

- **Coins move:** A transaction that spends from the wallet or pays it reaches the mempool, then confirms.
- **The daily scan finds coins gone:** Coins left the wallet without the server seeing the spend live.
- **A timelock opens:** A recovery path becomes spendable in 30 days, seven days, one day, and on the day itself.
- **The watch starts or stops:** The first scan of a wallet is done, or the server stops watching a wallet it [refuses](/premium/limits).
- **A device connects:** A new device uses your licence key, a device gets full access, or the licence key changes. See [Devices](/premium/devices).

## Pick where alerts go

Alerts reach the ntfy app, Telegram, an e-mail address or a webhook you run. See [Channels](/premium/channels). Gerfaut also lists the last 20 alerts in the `Recent alerts` card.

## Know what the server keeps

The server holds no private key and cannot move your coins. Under your licence key, it keeps:

- **Wallets:** The descriptor or address of each wallet you switch on, and its name.
- **Addresses:** Every address of those wallets, with the coins and movements seen on them.
- **Channels:** An ntfy topic, a Telegram chat, an e-mail address or a webhook URL.
- **Licence key:** A hash of it, never the licence key itself.
- **Devices:** The kind of each device you connect, such as `Android phone` or `Mac`, when it connected and was approved, and a hash of its own access token.
- **Refused wallets:** For one hour after refusing a wallet, a fingerprint of it keyed with a server secret, never the descriptor or address itself.

It keeps no e-mail unless you add one as a channel, no IP address and no request log. See [Delete your data](/premium/delete-your-data).

## Pay for time, not for an account

Prepay one month, six months or one year, in bitcoin over Ark, Lightning or on-chain. The [Premium page](https://gerfaut-wallet.com/premium) shows the prices. Paid time adds to the end of what you already have, and alerts continue for seven days after it ends.
