The apps show a balance when you open them, and nothing happens in between. Gerfaut Premium adds a server that watches the wallets you choose around the clock, from its own Bitcoin node, and tells you within seconds when coins move. You buy a key, enter it in the app, switch a wallet on: there is no account.

## Get told when something happens

The server sends an alert on these events:

- **Coins move:** A transaction spending from the wallet reaches the node's mempool, and a second alert follows when it confirms. Coins arriving are announced the same way.
- **The daily rescan finds something:** Once a day the server rescans the UTXO set and reports coins gone without the spend being seen live.
- **A timelock opens:** For a wallet with a recovery path behind a timelock, an alert 30 days, 7 days and 1 day before the path becomes spendable, then again the day it opens.
- **The watch starts:** The first scan of a wallet you switched on is done.

Alerts reach the ntfy app, Telegram, an e-mail address, or a webhook you run. An ntfy, Telegram or e-mail alert names the wallet and says what happened, and carries no address, no amount and no transaction id. A webhook posts to your own machine, so it carries the whole event. The app lists the last 20 alerts under `Recent alerts`, and shows a red banner on the home screen when the server itself stops answering.

## Know what the server keeps

Watch-only stays watch-only: the server holds no key, can see coins move and cannot move them.

Kept on the server:

- The descriptors of the wallets you register, and the names you gave them.
- The addresses derived from those descriptors, with the coins and movements seen on them.
- The channels you add: an ntfy topic, a Telegram link, an e-mail address or a webhook URL, and the name Telegram gives a chat you link.
- A hash of your key. The key itself is never stored.

Never kept: your e-mail unless you add one as a channel, your IP address, or any request log. Stop watching a wallet, or remove a watched wallet from the app, and its data leaves the server with it: see [Delete your data](/premium/delete-your-data).

The app talks to `api.gerfaut-wallet.com` over HTTPS. When your backend is a `.onion` address, those calls go through Tor too: see [Tor](/settings/tor).

## Pay for time, not for an account

One plan covers every service. You prepay time: 1 month for 5 €, 6 months for 24 € or 1 year for 36 €, paid in bitcoin over Ark, Lightning or on-chain. Paid time adds to the end of what you already have, and one key watches up to 21 wallets. After the paid time ends, alerts continue for 7 days, then stop until you add time.
