---
title: "Watch a wallet"
description: "Switch a wallet on so the Premium server follows it and alerts you, read its first scan and the alerts in Gerfaut, and stop the watch when you want."
---

Switch a mainnet wallet on, and the Premium server follows it around the clock: every address of a descriptor, present and future, or a single address. You need an active [Premium licence key](/premium/get-a-key) on a device with [full access](/premium/devices).

<Warning>
Switching a wallet on sends its descriptor, or its address, and its name to the Premium server. From then on, the server sees when coins move on the wallet.
</Warning>

## Switch a wallet on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Premium">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Desktop">Select **Settings** in the sidebar, then **Premium**.</Tab>
      <Tab title="Android">Select **More** > **Settings** > **Premium**.</Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the switch of the wallet">
    The `Watched wallets` card lists your mainnet wallets. The first time, `Watch this wallet from the server` says what leaves the device and what the server learns. Select **Watch this wallet**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the first scan">
    The row reads `First scan pending` for a few seconds to a few minutes. It then reads `Watched since` with the date and the number of coins, next to the `Watched` pill.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Each mainnet wallet has a switch under Watched wallets, and the alerts land under Recent alerts.">
  <img src="/images/android/settings-premium.webp" alt="The Premium section on Android with the Watched wallets, Channels and Recent alerts cards." width="360" />
</Frame>

<Note>
The server refuses a wallet that holds more than 5,000 coins. The row then shows the server's sentence, and nothing is watched. See [Limits](/premium/limits).
</Note>

## Read the alerts in Gerfaut

The `Recent alerts` card lists the last 20 alerts, newest first, such as `coins are moving` or `coins are gone`. Select a line to open the wallet. The same alerts reach every [channel](/premium/channels) you added.

## Watch for the offline banner

Gerfaut checks that the Premium server answers when it opens and every 15 minutes. After two misses in a row, a red banner appears on the Overview and on the Android home screen. It reads `Gerfaut's watch is offline since` with the time, then `Your wallets are not being monitored.` Select **Acknowledge** to hide it for 24 hours.

## Stop watching a wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the switch of the wallet again">
    An amber note says that unwatching also deletes the wallet's alert history on the server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Unwatch">
    Confirm with your app lock, as in [Confirm sensitive actions](/premium/devices#confirm-sensitive-actions). The switch turns off once the server answers. Switching the wallet on again asks for your consent again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Remove the wallet from Gerfaut altogether">
    Removing a wallet in **Settings** > **Wallets** removes it from the server too, with its alerts. When the device is offline, Gerfaut sends the request at the next contact. The server keeps alerting until then.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Unwatch a wallet this device no longer holds">
    A wallet you switched on from another device has a row that says it is still watched by the server. Select **Unwatch**, then confirm under the row.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
