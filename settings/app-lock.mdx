---
title: "App lock"
description: "Lock Gerfaut behind a PIN or a password, unlock it with biometrics on Android, and hide it behind a working calculator on your launcher."
---

Lock Gerfaut so that someone at your unlocked computer or phone sees nothing of your wallets. Your wallet list stays encrypted whether the lock is on or off.

## Turn on the app lock

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the App lock switch">
    Open **Settings** > **Security**. The dialog `Turn on the app lock` opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick PIN or Password">
    Under `Kind`, select `PIN` (four to 12 digits) or `Password` (at least eight characters). Only a PIN opens the calculator disguise on Android.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the secret twice">
    Enter `New PIN` or `New password`, then `Confirm`, and select **Turn on**. Gerfaut asks for the secret each time it opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Lock and unlock

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Press `Ctrl+L`, select **Lock** at the bottom of the sidebar, or select **Lock now** in **Settings** > **Security**. Otherwise Gerfaut stays unlocked until you close it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Gerfaut locks each time it goes to the background. Select **Lock now** in **Settings** > **Security** to lock it by hand.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

On the `Locked` screen, enter the secret and select **Unlock**. After three wrong attempts, Gerfaut makes you wait five seconds, then doubles the wait at each failure, up to five minutes.

## Change or turn off the lock

Select **Change PIN** or **Change password** to replace the secret. Select the `App lock` switch again to turn the lock off. Both ask for the current secret.

## Unlock with biometrics on Android

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the Unlock with biometrics switch">
    The switch shows when the phone can read a fingerprint or a face.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your PIN or password, then confirm the system prompt">
    The lock screen then offers **Use fingerprint or face**. Gerfaut never sees the biometric itself.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Disguise the app as a calculator on Android

<Warning>
With the disguise on, the PIN is the only way into Gerfaut. If you forget it, only a [backup](/settings/backup-and-sync) brings your wallets back into a fresh install.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Disguise the app">
    It needs a PIN lock. The sheet lists what changes: the launcher shows `Calculator` instead of Gerfaut, and notifications, widgets and [Live](/settings/notifications) turn off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Turn on the disguise">
    Clear your recent apps once, because Android may keep an older Gerfaut thumbnail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Gerfaut from the calculator">
    Type your PIN on the calculator and press `=`. A wrong PIN gives a number like any calculator, with no hint.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To drop the disguise, select the `Disguise the app` switch again, or turn the app lock off.
