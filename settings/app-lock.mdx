---
title: "App lock"
description: "Lock Gerfaut behind a PIN or a password, unlock it with biometrics on Android, and hide it behind a working calculator on your launcher."
---

The app lock stops someone at your unlocked computer or holding your unlocked phone. It replaces the whole of Gerfaut rather than covering it, so nothing of a wallet shows before you unlock.

Open **Settings** > **Security** and find the `Security` card.

<Tip>
The vault that holds your wallet list stays encrypted whether the lock is on or off.
</Tip>

## Turn on the app lock

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the App lock switch">
    The dialog `Turn on the app lock` opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the kind of secret">
    Under `Kind`, select `PIN` (four to 12 digits) or `Password` (at least eight characters). Only a PIN can open the calculator disguise on Android.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the secret twice">
    Enter `New PIN` or `New password`, then `Confirm`, and select **Turn on**. Gerfaut asks for the secret when it opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Lock and unlock

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    The window stays unlocked until you close it. To lock it before then, press `Ctrl+L`, select **Lock** at the bottom of the sidebar, or select **Lock now** in the `Security` card.

    <Frame caption="The app lock protects an unlocked computer. The vault stays encrypted whether the lock is on or off.">
      <img src="/images/desktop/settings-lock.webp" alt="The Security section of the desktop settings: the App lock switch, off, with its explanation." />
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Gerfaut asks again every time it comes back from the background. A file picker or a share sheet that Gerfaut opened itself does not count. Select **Lock now** in the `Security` card to lock it by hand.

    <Frame caption="Gerfaut asks for the secret at every open and every return from the background.">
      <img src="/images/android/settings-lock.webp" alt="The Security section on Android with the App lock switch, off, and its explanation." width="360" />
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The lock screen reads `Locked`. Enter the secret and select **Unlock**.

- **Wrong attempts:** After three, Gerfaut waits five seconds before the next one, doubles the wait at each failure up to five minutes, and counts it down on screen. Restarting Gerfaut does not shorten it.
- **Android:** A lock also keeps the task switcher and screenshots from capturing Gerfaut.

## Change or turn off the lock

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Change PIN or Change password">
    The dialog asks for the current secret, then the new one twice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the App lock switch to turn the lock off">
    The dialog `Turn off the app lock` asks for the secret once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Unlock with biometrics on Android

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the Unlock with biometrics switch">
    The `Security` card shows it when the phone can ask for a fingerprint or a face.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your PIN or password, then confirm the system prompt">
    The lock screen then offers **Use fingerprint or face** and fires the prompt on its own. Gerfaut never sees the biometric itself.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Disguise the app as a calculator on Android

<Warning>
Forget the PIN and Gerfaut cannot be opened: with the disguise on, the PIN is the only way in. A backup made with Gerfaut brings the wallets back into a fresh install, see [Backup and sync](/settings/backup-and-sync).
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Disguise the app">
    It needs a PIN lock in place. The sheet says what changes. The launcher shows a calculator named `Calculator` instead of Gerfaut, notifications and home-screen widgets turn off, and `Settings` > `Apps` on the phone still lists Gerfaut.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Turn on the disguise">
    Then clear your recent apps once, because Android may keep an older Gerfaut thumbnail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Gerfaut from the calculator">
    Type your PIN on the calculator and press `=`. A wrong PIN shows the number like any calculator would, with no hint. The calculator is real: four operations, percentage, sign and decimal point.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Accordion title="Drop the disguise">
  Open **Settings** > **Security** and select the `Disguise the app` switch again, or turn the app lock off. Both bring Gerfaut back to the launcher.
</Accordion>
