---
title: "App lock"
description: "Lock Gerfaut behind a PIN or a password, unlock it with biometrics on Android, and hide it behind a working calculator on your launcher."
---

Lock Gerfaut so that someone at your unlocked computer or phone sees nothing of your wallets. Your wallet list stays encrypted whether the lock is on or off.

## Turn on the app lock

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the App lock switch">
    Open **Settings** > **Security**. Gerfaut opens `Turn on the app lock`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick PIN or Password">
    Select `PIN` (four to 12 digits) or `Password` (at least eight characters). Only a PIN opens the calculator disguise on Android.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the secret twice">
    Enter `New PIN` or `New password`, then `Confirm`, and select **Turn on**. Gerfaut asks for the secret each time it opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Lock and unlock

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Press `Ctrl+L`, select **Lock** at the bottom of the sidebar, or select **Lock now** in **Settings** > **Security**. Otherwise Gerfaut stays unlocked until you close it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Gerfaut asks for the secret each time you come back to it. Returning within ten minutes from a file picker, a save dialog or a share sheet that Gerfaut opened needs no secret. Select **Lock now** in **Settings** > **Security** to lock it by hand. While a lock is set, Android keeps Gerfaut out of screenshots and shows a blank card in the recent apps, or the calculator under the disguise.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

On the `Locked` screen, enter the secret and select **Unlock**. After three wrong attempts, Gerfaut makes you wait five seconds, then doubles the wait at each failure, up to five minutes.

## Change or turn off the lock

Select **Change PIN** or **Change password** to replace the secret. Select the `App lock` switch again to turn the lock off. Both ask for the current secret.

## Unlock with biometrics on Android

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the Unlock with biometrics switch">
    The switch shows when the phone can read a fingerprint or a face.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your PIN or password, then confirm the system prompt">
    The lock screen then offers **Use fingerprint or face**. Gerfaut never sees the biometric itself.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Disguise the app as a calculator on Android

<Warning>
With the disguise on, the PIN is the only way into Gerfaut. If you forget it, only a [backup](#start-over-after-a-forgotten-pin-or-password) brings your wallets back.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Disguise the app">
    It needs an app lock of the `PIN` kind. The sheet lists what changes, starting with the launcher, which shows `Calculator` instead of Gerfaut.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Turn on the disguise">
    Clear your recent apps once, because Android may keep an older Gerfaut thumbnail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Gerfaut from the calculator">
    Type your PIN on the calculator and press `=`. A wrong PIN gives a number like any calculator, with no hint. After three wrong PINs, the right one also gives a number until the [wait](#lock-and-unlock) ends.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Turning the disguise on changes how Gerfaut tells you about transactions:

- **Notifications:** Gerfaut posts none, and clears the ones already in the notification shade.
- **Live:** If Live is on, Gerfaut turns it off and sets `Check for transactions` to `Every 15 min`. Background checks post nothing either.
- **Widgets:** Gerfaut removes its widgets from your home screen.

In **Settings** > **Notifications**, `New transactions` shows off and both settings stay greyed out while the disguise is on. The line under the switch says why. Once you turn the disguise off, `New transactions` is back as you left it.

## Turn off the disguise

Select the `Disguise the app` switch again, or turn the app lock off. Gerfaut does not restore Live or the widgets on its own:

- **Live:** If you used [Live](/settings/notifications#turn-on-notifications), select `Live` again under `Check for transactions` in **Settings** > **Notifications**.
- **Widgets:** The disguise removed them from your home screen. [Add each one again](/settings/notifications#put-a-widget-on-your-android-home-screen).

## Start over after a forgotten PIN or password

Nobody can reset a forgotten PIN or password, since Gerfaut keeps no copy of it. Start Gerfaut again on an empty vault, then restore your last [backup](/settings/backup-and-sync#restore-a-backup).

<Note>
Only the wallets in your backup come back. Add any other wallet again from its descriptor, extended public key or address.
</Note>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Close Gerfaut">
        Close every Gerfaut window.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Rename the Gerfaut data folder">
        Rename `com.gerfautwallet.gerfaut`, for example to `com.gerfautwallet.gerfaut.old`. Gerfaut keeps it here:

        - **Windows:** `%APPDATA%\com.gerfautwallet.gerfaut`
        - **macOS:** `~/Library/Application Support/com.gerfautwallet.gerfaut`
        - **Linux:** `~/.local/share/com.gerfautwallet.gerfaut`
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open Gerfaut and restore the backup">
        Gerfaut opens on an empty vault, with no lock. Follow [Restore a backup](/settings/backup-and-sync#restore-a-backup).
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Uninstall Gerfaut">
        In the Android settings, open the list of apps, select Gerfaut and uninstall it.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Install Gerfaut again">
        Follow [Install on Android](/getting-started/install-android).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Restore the backup">
        Follow [Restore a backup](/settings/backup-and-sync#restore-a-backup).
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
