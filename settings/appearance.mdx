title: "Appearance"
description: "Switch Gerfaut between its light theme, its dark theme and the theme of your system, and make room on desktop by collapsing the sidebar."
---

Gerfaut opens in its light theme. A dark theme and a setting that follows your system are one selection away.

Open **Settings** > **General** and find the `Appearance` card.

## Pick a theme

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a theme under Theme">
    Select `Light`, `Dark` or `System`. The theme changes at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let System follow your operating system">
    `System` follows the theme of your operating system as it changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Collapse the sidebar on desktop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Collapse sidebar">
    The button sits in the top right of the sidebar. The sidebar shrinks to its icons, and each one keeps its label as a tooltip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Expand sidebar to bring the labels back">
    Gerfaut remembers the choice.
  </Step>
</Steps>
