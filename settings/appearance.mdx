---
title: "Appearance"
description: "Switch Gerfaut between its light theme, its dark theme and the theme of your system, and make room on the desktop by collapsing the sidebar."
---

Gerfaut opens in its light theme. A dark theme and a setting that follows the system are one selection away.

## Pick a theme

Open **Settings** > **General**. In the `Appearance` card, under `Theme`, select `Light`, `Dark` or `System`. The theme changes at once. `System` follows the theme of your operating system as it changes.

## Collapse the sidebar on desktop

Select **Collapse sidebar** in the top right of the sidebar. The sidebar shrinks to its icons; each one keeps its label as a tooltip. Select **Expand sidebar** to bring the labels back. Gerfaut remembers the choice.
