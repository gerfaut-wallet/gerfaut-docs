title: "Appearance"
description: "Switch Gerfaut between its light theme, its dark theme and the theme of your system, and make room on desktop by collapsing the sidebar."
---

Switch Gerfaut to its dark theme, or let it follow your system. Open **Settings** > **General** and find the `Appearance` card.

## Pick a theme

Under `Theme`, select `Light` (the default), `Dark` or `System`. `System` follows your operating system as it changes.

## Collapse the sidebar on desktop

Select **Collapse sidebar** at the top right of the sidebar. The sidebar keeps its icons, with each label as a tooltip. Select **Expand sidebar** to bring the labels back.
