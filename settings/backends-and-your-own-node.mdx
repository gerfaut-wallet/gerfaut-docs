Point Gerfaut at your own node to keep your addresses to yourself, or stay on the public servers. Each network keeps its own backend.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">Select **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Network**.</Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">Select **More**, then **Settings**, then **Network**.</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Pick the network

In the `Network` card, select `Mainnet`, `Signet`, `Testnet 4` or `Regtest`. Gerfaut shows only the wallets of the selected network and names it in a badge everywhere except on Mainnet.

<Tip>
Regtest has no public server: run your own node and point Gerfaut at it, as below.
</Tip>

## Use the public servers

The `Backend` card opens on `Public API`: free servers with nothing to set up.

<Warning>
The operator of the public server that answers sees the addresses of your wallets.
</Warning>

Under `Server`, pick who answers:

- **`Automatic`:** Gerfaut tries every public Esplora instance of the network in turn until one answers.
- **One operator:** Select it to make it the only server asked, such as `mempool.space`, `blockstream.info` or `mempool.emzy.de` over Esplora, or one of the listed Electrum servers.
- **Self-signed Electrum servers:** The list says which ones sign their own certificate. Gerfaut shows you the fingerprint before it connects, see [Certificates](/settings/certificates).
- **Single-address wallets:** An Electrum server cannot serve a wallet added from a single address. Keep such a wallet on `Automatic` or on an Esplora server.

## Connect your own Esplora

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select My own Esplora">
    The `Server URL` field appears, for an address such as `https://node.example.org:3002/api`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the URL, or scan it">
    Select **Scan** beside the field to read the QR code your node prints beside its Esplora app. The scan fills the field and saves nothing yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Save backend">
    The setting applies to the next sync.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect your own Electrum server

<Warning>
Leave `TLS` on. Without it everything travels in the clear, and anything on the path between your device and the server reads the addresses you watch.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select My own Electrum server">
    Use it for electrs or Fulcrum, over TLS or plain TCP.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the host and the port">
    Enter the `Host`, such as `node.example.org` or an `.onion` address, and the `Port`, `50002` by default. Or select **Scan** and point the camera at the QR code your node prints beside its Electrum app: it fills the host, the port and the `TLS` switch at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Save backend">
    The button reads `Checking the certificate…` while Gerfaut checks the certificate. A server that signs its own certificate shows it to you before anything is saved, see [Certificates](/settings/certificates). A server that does not answer is saved anyway, and Gerfaut checks its certificate on the first connection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Reach your node over Tor">
    A `Host` that ends in `.onion` goes through Tor. The `Tor` card under the backend says which Tor client Gerfaut uses and lets you test it, see [Tor](/settings/tor).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Scan a QR code of the other kind">
    A scanned QR code that names the other kind of backend switches the choice for you: an `https://` address read into the Electrum form selects `My own Esplora`.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
