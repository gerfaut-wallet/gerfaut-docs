---
title: "Backends and your own node"
description: "Choose which network Gerfaut shows and where it reads the chain: the public servers, your own Esplora endpoint or your own Electrum server."
---

Gerfaut reads the chain from public servers by default. Point it at your own node over Esplora or Electrum to keep your addresses to yourself. Each network has its own backend.

Open the settings: on desktop, select **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar; on Android, select **More**, then **Settings**. Then open **Network**.

## Pick the network

The `Network` card offers `Mainnet`, `Signet`, `Testnet 4` and `Regtest`. Only the wallets of the selected network are shown, and a badge names the network everywhere except on Mainnet.

## Use the public servers

The `Backend` card opens on `Public API`: free, keyless servers with no setup. The operator that answers can see the addresses of your wallets.

Under `Server`, `Automatic` tries every public Esplora instance of the network in turn until one answers. Select an operator instead to make it the only server asked, for instance `mempool.space`, `blockstream.info` or `mempool.emzy.de` over Esplora, or one of the listed Electrum servers. The list says which Electrum servers sign their own certificate: Gerfaut shows you the fingerprint before it connects, see [Certificates](/settings/certificates).

<Note>
An Electrum server cannot serve a wallet added from a single address. Keep such a wallet on `Automatic` or on an Esplora server.
</Note>

Regtest has no public server: run your own node and point Gerfaut at it.

## Connect your own Esplora

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select My own Esplora">
    The field `Server URL` appears, such as `https://node.example.org:3002/api`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the URL, or scan it">
    Select **Scan** beside the field to read the QR code your node prints beside its Esplora app. The scan fills the field; nothing is saved yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Save backend">
    The setting applies to the next sync.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect your own Electrum server

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select My own Electrum server">
    For electrs or Fulcrum, reachable over TLS or plain TCP.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the host and the port">
    Enter the `Host`, such as `node.example.org` or an `.onion` address, and the `Port`, `50002` by default. Or select **Scan** and point the camera at the QR code your node prints beside its Electrum app: it fills the host, the port and the `TLS` switch at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave TLS on">
    Without TLS everything travels in the clear: anything on the path between the device and the server reads the addresses being watched.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Save backend">
    Gerfaut checks the certificate first and the button reads `Checking the certificate…`. A server that signs its own certificate shows it to you before anything is saved: see [Certificates](/settings/certificates). A server that does not answer is saved anyway, and its certificate is checked on the first connection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An address that ends in `.onion` goes through Tor. The `Tor` card below the backend says which Tor client Gerfaut uses and lets you test it: see [Tor](/settings/tor).

<Tip>
A scanned QR code that names the other kind of backend switches the choice for you: an `https://` address read into the Electrum form selects `My own Esplora`.
</Tip>
