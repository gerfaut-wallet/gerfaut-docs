---
title: "Notifications"
description: "Get a notification when a sync finds a new transaction, check your wallets on a schedule, and put the price or a balance on your Android home screen."
---

Gerfaut tells you when a sync finds a transaction you have not seen. The notification comes from the sync itself, which talks to the same servers as always and nothing else.

Open **Settings** > **Notifications**. Both settings are off by default.

## Get notified about new transactions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the New transactions switch">
    The system asks for permission the first time. If it refuses, the card reads `Notifications are off for Gerfaut in the system settings.`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the notification">
    It names the wallet and says `Received 0.01000000 BTC` or `0.00200000 BTC left this wallet`, with `· pending` while the transaction has no block. While amounts are hidden, it only says `New transaction`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Gerfaut sends at most three notifications per wallet per sync, then one line that counts the rest.
</Tip>

## Check on a schedule

The schedule stays greyed out while `New transactions` is off.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Under `Check while open`, select `5 min`, `15 min` or `1 hour`. Gerfaut syncs every wallet on that rhythm while its window is open.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Under `Check in the background`, select `Every 15 min`, `Every hour` or `Every 6 hours`. Gerfaut syncs even when it is closed. Android checks at most every 15 minutes and may delay a check to save battery.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Put a widget on your Android home screen

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hold an empty spot on your home screen">
    Select **Widgets** and pick Gerfaut.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick one of the three widgets">
    - **Bitcoin price:** The price in your chosen currency, as of the last refresh.
    - **Balance:** The total of your wallets and up to four of them by name. Balances stay masked as `•••••` until you tick `Show balances on widgets` in the `Widgets` card, and whenever amounts are hidden in Gerfaut.
    - **Network:** The block height as of the last sync.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Home-screen widgets show the price, the block height and the wallet balances, masked by default.">
  <img src="/images/android/widget.webp" alt="An Android launcher with the three Gerfaut widgets: Bitcoin price, Balance with masked amounts per wallet, and Network with the block height." width="360" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
Nothing private reaches a widget: a name, a balance, a price, a height, never an address or a transaction id.
</Tip>

<Accordion title="When a widget refreshes">
  A widget never syncs on its own. It refreshes when you open Gerfaut and with the background check above. Widgets are off while Gerfaut is disguised as a calculator, see [App lock](/settings/app-lock).
</Accordion>
