---
title: "Notifications"
description: "Get a notification from Gerfaut when a sync finds a new transaction, check your wallets on a schedule, and put the price or a balance on your Android home screen."
---

Gerfaut can tell you when a sync finds a transaction you have not seen. The notification comes from the sync itself: it talks to the same servers as always, and nothing else.

Open **Settings** > **Notifications**. Both settings are off by default.

## Get notified about new transactions

Select the `New transactions` switch. The system asks for permission the first time; if it refuses, the card reads `Notifications are off for Gerfaut in the system settings.`

A notification names the wallet and says `Received 0.01000000 BTC` or `0.00200000 BTC left this wallet`, with `· pending` while the transaction has no block. While amounts are hidden, it only says `New transaction`. Gerfaut sends at most three per wallet per sync, then one line that counts the rest.

## Check on a schedule

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Under `Check while open`, select `5 min`, `15 min` or `1 hour`. Gerfaut syncs every wallet on that rhythm while its window is open.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Under `Check in the background`, select `Every 15 min`, `Every hour` or `Every 6 hours`. Gerfaut syncs even when it is closed. Android checks at most every 15 minutes and may delay a check to save battery.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The schedule stays greyed out while `New transactions` is off.

## Put a widget on your Android home screen

Hold an empty spot on your home screen, select **Widgets** and pick Gerfaut. Three widgets exist:

- **Bitcoin price:** The price in your chosen currency, as of the last refresh.
- **Balance:** The total of your wallets and up to four of them by name. Balances stay masked as `•••••` until you select `Show balances on widgets` in the `Widgets` card, and whenever amounts are hidden in the app.
- **Network:** The block height as of the last sync.

A widget never syncs on its own: it refreshes when you open Gerfaut and with the background check above. Nothing private reaches it: a name, a balance, a price, a height, never an address or a transaction id. Widgets are off while the app is disguised as a calculator.
