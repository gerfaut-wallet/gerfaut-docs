title: "Notifications"
description: "Get notified when a transaction reaches the mempool and when it confirms, see when each check runs, let Live follow every address and add a widget."
---

Gerfaut sends these notifications on its own, with no Gerfaut server in between. It watches the wallets of the network selected in **Settings** > **Network**.

<Warning>
While notifications run, your backend's operator sees your addresses, how long Gerfaut stays connected and each IP address it connects from. With the `Automatic` server, Gerfaut first connects to the Electrum server of an operator it already syncs with, Blockstream on mainnet. Point Gerfaut at [your own node](/settings/backends-and-your-own-node) to keep all of it to yourself.
</Warning>

## Turn on notifications

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select the New transactions switch">
        Open **Settings** > **Notifications**. The system may ask you to allow notifications.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Read the Live watch line">
        The line reads `Connected · Electrum` or `Connected · mempool WebSocket`, followed by the server. Another Esplora backend reads `Polling every minute` instead.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select Send a test notification">
        A test notification appears. If nothing shows, allow notifications for Gerfaut in the system settings and turn off Do Not Disturb.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    In step 2, pick how Gerfaut checks:

    - **`Live`:** A connection kept open, which brings a notification within seconds.
    - **`Every 15 min`, `Every hour` or `Every 6 hours`:** Android checks in the background to save battery, sometimes later than asked.
    - **`Off`:** Gerfaut checks only while it is open.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select the New transactions switch">
        Open **Settings** > **Notifications**. Android asks you to allow notifications the first time.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select Live under Check for transactions">
        The `Live watch` sheet says what Live costs and what your server learns. Select **Turn on Live**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Let Gerfaut run in the background">
        Select **Ask Android** and allow Gerfaut to ignore battery optimisation. Live still runs if you refuse, less reliably.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Follow the steps for your phone">
        On a Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO, Huawei, Honor, Samsung, OnePlus, Oppo or realme phone, the sheet lists what to change in the phone's own battery manager. Change it, then select **Done**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Read the status line">
        The line under `Check for transactions` reads `Connected · Electrum` or `Connected · mempool`, followed by the server. Another Esplora backend reads `Polling every minute`. Android shows a permanent notification, `Watching for transactions`.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    If `Notifications are off for Gerfaut in the system settings.` shows under `New transactions`, select **Open system settings** and allow notifications. Live waits meanwhile, and starts again when you come back to Gerfaut.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Check when notifications arrive

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Gerfaut notifies you within seconds while it runs, open, minimised or locked. Once you close it, nothing arrives. When you open it again, its first sync notifies you of what came in meanwhile.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    | `Check for transactions` | When a notification arrives | What stops it |
    |---|---|---|
    | `Live` | Within seconds, with Gerfaut open, in the background, swiped away or after a phone restart. | Force-stopping Gerfaut, until you open it again. |
    | `Every 15 min`, `Every hour`, `Every 6 hours` | At the next background check, which Android runs at most every 15 minutes, once the phone is online and its battery is not low. | Force-stopping Gerfaut, until you open it again. |
    | `Off` | When Gerfaut syncs while open, at launch or when you sync by hand. | Leaving Gerfaut. |

    While Gerfaut is [disguised as a calculator](/settings/app-lock#disguise-the-app-as-a-calculator-on-android), no notification arrives, whatever the mode. Gerfaut Premium alerts still reach your [channels](/premium/channels).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Within seconds holds for the addresses an Electrum server or a mempool instance pushes to Live. Other addresses wait longer, as below.

## Make Live follow every address

Live follows up to 200 addresses per wallet and 2,000 in all, or 20,000 on [your own node](/settings/backends-and-your-own-node#follow-more-addresses-on-your-own-node). A payment to any other address shows at the next sync: when Gerfaut opens, when you sync by hand, or at the complete sync Live runs once a day.

In each wallet, Live takes the addresses most likely to move first:

- **Coins:** The addresses that hold coins, where a spend shows.
- **Receive addresses:** The unused ones Gerfaut showed last, as many as the [gap limit](/wallets/add-a-wallet#manage-your-wallets), then as many again past the last one shown.
- **Change addresses:** The same, on the change side.
- **The rest:** Older addresses, newest first.

Between wallets, Live takes the first address of each wallet, then the second, and so on. [Pinned wallets](#pin-the-wallets-live-follows-first) come first, then the wallets that hold coins. A server can also refuse some addresses, which then wait for the next sync too.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Use Live with an Esplora backend">
    An Esplora backend pushes changes for fewer addresses than an Electrum server. Each minute, Gerfaut reads a few of the other addresses, in turn:

    - **A mempool instance:** A public instance, such as mempool.space, pushes changes for ten addresses per connection on mainnet, and more than 1,000 on signet and testnet4. Gerfaut reads three of the others each minute. With N addresses taking turns, each one is read about every N/3 minutes.
    - **Another Esplora backend:** It pushes nothing. Each minute, Gerfaut reads the two addresses Live takes first, and one of the others. With N addresses taking turns, each one is read about every N minutes.
    - **Your own node:** Gerfaut reads 30 addresses a minute instead of three, so each one about every N/30 minutes on a mempool instance. On another Esplora backend, the first two still come every minute and 28 take turns, so about every N/28 minutes.
    - **A server that limits requests:** Each time it does, Gerfaut waits twice as long between two reads, up to ten minutes. It goes back to once a minute half an hour after the last limit.

    With 300 addresses taking turns, each one waits about 100 minutes between two reads on a mempool instance, and about 300 minutes on another Esplora backend. The next sync reads all of them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Use Live with a server that refuses addresses">
    An Electrum server may take fewer addresses than Live asks it to follow. The others wait for the next sync, until Gerfaut asks that server for all of them again:

    - **After a refusal:** Gerfaut asks again a day later, or an hour later when the server took none.
    - **After ElectrumX cuts Live off:** ElectrumX does so when the list costs it too much. Gerfaut then asks it for fewer addresses until you change the backend in **Settings** > **Network**, or turn Live off and on again.

    On your own node, [raise the subscription limit](/settings/backends-and-your-own-node#raise-the-subscription-limit-of-your-electrum-server) to follow every address.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## See which wallets wait for the next sync

When Live cannot follow every address, a note under the Live status in **Settings** > **Notifications** says how many addresses of how many wallets wait for the next sync. Off your own node, the note points to `This is my node` in **Settings** > **Network**. On your own node, it names the setting to raise when the node refuses addresses.

Each wallet then carries a badge:

- **`Live`:** Live follows every address of the wallet.
- **`Partly live`:** Live follows the addresses that come first in [its order](#make-live-follow-every-address). The others wait for the next sync.
- **`At next sync`:** Live follows none of the addresses of the wallet. A payment to it shows at the next sync.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    The badge shows on the `Live watch` row of the `Watch status` card, on the wallet's Overview, with a count under it such as `40 addresses wait for the next sync.` It also shows on the wallet's row in **Settings** > **Wallets**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    The badge shows on the wallet's card on the home screen and on its row in **Settings** > **Wallets**. On the wallet page, it sits under the sync line, next to a count such as `40 addresses wait for the next sync.` The permanent notification reads `Connected to your server · some addresses wait for syncs`.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Pin the wallets Live follows first

When Live cannot follow every address, it follows the wallets you pin before the others.

<Note>
Pinned wallets go before every other wallet. Pinning several large wallets can leave the others to the syncs.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Wallets">
    Select **Advanced** under the wallet list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the switch of each wallet to pin">
    The switches sit under `Always watch live first`. Live picks its addresses again at once. Folded, the button reads `Advanced · 1 wallet watched live first`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keep Live running on Android

Live keeps running when you leave Gerfaut, swipe it away from the recent apps or restart the phone. A check every 15 minutes stays scheduled under it, for whenever Live stops.

- **Stop:** Select **Stop** on the permanent notification to turn Live off.
- **Force stop:** Force-stopping Gerfaut in the Android settings ends Live until you open Gerfaut again.
- **Tor:** With a `.onion` backend, the built-in Tor client stays active for as long as Live runs, which costs battery.
- **Disguise:** Turning on the [calculator disguise](/settings/app-lock#disguise-the-app-as-a-calculator-on-android) stops Live. Select `Live` again once you [turn the disguise off](/settings/app-lock#turn-off-the-disguise).
- **`Stopped by Android. Tap to restart.`:** Select the line to start Live again.
- **`Battery: restricted`:** Select **Fix** to ask Android for the battery exemption again.

## Read a notification

The notification names the wallet and says what happened, such as `Received 0.01000000 BTC · pending`, then `Received 0.01000000 BTC · confirmed`. Gerfaut sends at most three notifications per wallet at once, then one that counts the rest.

- **Hidden amounts:** While [amounts are hidden](/everyday-use/balances-and-history#hide-every-amount), a notification reads like `New transaction · pending`, with no amount.
- **App lock:** A notification names no wallet and no amount, on desktop while Gerfaut is locked and on Android whenever an app lock is set.
- **Payment no longer coming:** When an incoming payment leaves the mempool before it confirms, and nothing pays the wallet in its place, a notification says so, such as `A pending payment of 0.00150000 BTC is no longer coming`. Gerfaut says so only once two syncs, at least ten minutes apart, have both missed it.
- **Fee bump:** A payment Gerfaut already announced brings no new notification when its sender bumps the fee.

## Put a widget on your Android home screen

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hold an empty spot on your home screen">
    Select **Widgets** and find Gerfaut.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag one of the three widgets">
    - **Bitcoin price:** The price in your currency.
    - **Balance:** The total of your wallets and up to four of them by name.
    - **Network:** The block height of the last sync.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Home-screen widgets show the price, the block height and the wallet balances, masked by default.">
  <img src="/images/android/widget.webp" alt="An Android launcher with the three Gerfaut widgets: Bitcoin price, Balance with masked amounts per wallet, and Network with the block height." width="360" />
</Frame>

The `Bitcoin price` widget says when Gerfaut fetched the price, such as `as of 09:41`. A price from an earlier day shows its date too, such as `as of Oct 03, 09:41`, or `as of Oct 03` when the widget is too narrow for the time. With a `.onion` backend, the widget [never starts Tor](/settings/tor#route-other-requests-through-tor) for the price alone.

The `Balance` widget shows `•••••` until you tick `Show balances on widgets` in the `Widgets` card of **Settings** > **Notifications**. While an [app lock](/settings/app-lock) is set, it shows no wallet name and no amount, and the switch is greyed out.

A widget refreshes when you open Gerfaut and at each background check, and never shows an address or a transaction id. The widgets are off while Gerfaut is disguised as a calculator.
