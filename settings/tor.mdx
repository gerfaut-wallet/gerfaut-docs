---
title: "Tor"
description: "Reach a backend on a .onion address through the Tor client built into Gerfaut or the one already on your device, then test the route."
---

Gerfaut reaches a backend whose address ends in `.onion` through Tor, and every other address directly. Both apps carry their own Tor client, so a hidden service works with nothing else to install.

Open **Settings** > **Network** and find the `Tor` card under the backend.

## Pick which Tor Gerfaut uses

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    - **`Automatic`:** The Tor already running on this machine if it answers, the built-in one otherwise. This is the default.
    - **`System Tor`:** Only the Tor on this machine, at the `SOCKS address` you enter. Empty means `127.0.0.1:9050`, and the Tor Browser listens on `9150`.
    - **`Built-in`:** Only the built-in Tor. The first connection takes a little longer while it starts.

    <Frame caption="Any .onion backend goes through Tor, either the one already on the machine or the built-in one.">
      <img src="/images/desktop/settings-tor.webp" alt="The Tor card of the Network section on desktop: Automatic, System Tor and Built-in, the SOCKS address field and the Test the connection button." />
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    - **`Automatic`:** The built-in Tor. This is the default. Gerfaut uses a Tor app on the phone only when you select it, because on a phone any app can answer on the Tor port and pose as Tor.
    - **`System Tor`:** Only a Tor app running on the phone, such as Orbot.
    - **`Built-in`:** Only the built-in Tor. The first connection takes a little longer while it starts.

    <Frame caption="Any .onion backend goes through Tor, either built in or the one already on the device.">
      <img src="/images/android/settings-tor.webp" alt="The Tor card on Android: the Automatic, System Tor and Built-in buttons and the Test the connection button." width="360" />
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Test the route

<Steps>
  <Step title="Point the backend at your node's .onion address">
    In [Backend](/settings/backends-and-your-own-node), a scanned `.onion` address reads `A Tor hidden service: reached through Tor only.` under the field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Test the connection">
    While the built-in client starts, the card reads `Starting the built-in Tor…` with a percentage. A cold start takes up to about a minute and a half.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the result">
    The card reads `Reached through the built-in Tor.` or `Reached through the system Tor.`, or the error in Gerfaut's own words.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Accordion title="Send the Premium calls through Tor">
  The Premium calls follow the backend you set. With a `.onion` backend they go through Tor as well, with any other backend they go out directly. A call that needs Tor and cannot reach it fails, and never falls back to the clear.
</Accordion>
