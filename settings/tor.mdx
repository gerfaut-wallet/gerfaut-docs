title: "Tor"

---

Gerfaut reaches a backend whose address ends in `.onion` through Tor, with a Tor client built in, so a hidden service works with nothing else to install. Open **Settings** > **Network** and find the `Tor` card under the backend.

## Pick which Tor Gerfaut uses

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    - **`Automatic`:** The Tor already running on this machine if it answers, the built-in one otherwise. This is the default.
    - **`System Tor`:** Only the Tor on this machine, at the `SOCKS address` you enter. Empty means `127.0.0.1:9050`. The Tor Browser listens on `9150`.
    - **`Built-in`:** Only the built-in Tor.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    - **`Automatic`:** Only the built-in Tor, because any app on a phone can answer on the Tor port. This is the default.
    - **`System Tor`:** Only a Tor app running on the phone, such as Orbot.
    - **`Built-in`:** Only the built-in Tor.

## Test the route

<Steps>
  <Step title="Point the backend at your node's .onion address">
    Follow [Backend](/settings/backends-and-your-own-node) and enter the `.onion` address of your node.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Test the connection">
    The built-in Tor takes up to about a minute and a half to start the first time. Meanwhile, the card counts up in steps of ten, such as `Starting the built-in Tor… 40%`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the result">
    The card reads `Reached through the built-in Tor.` or `Reached through the system Tor.`, or says what failed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Route other requests through Tor

With a `.onion` backend, Gerfaut sends these requests through Tor too, and never around it:

- **Update check:** It goes through Tor as soon as one of your backends is a `.onion` address. When Tor cannot be reached, Gerfaut sends nothing.
- **Notifications:** The live connection goes to the backend itself, through Tor.
- **Price:** The fiat value, and the price chart on desktop, go through Tor as soon as one of your backends is a `.onion` address. When Tor cannot be reached, the price line reads `Tor is not available, so no price was asked.` Amounts then show without fiat.
- **Price widget on Android:** It never starts Tor for the price alone. While Gerfaut is closed, it fetches the price only through a Tor that already runs, such as the built-in one a sync started, or Orbot under `System Tor`. Otherwise it keeps the last price, with its date.
