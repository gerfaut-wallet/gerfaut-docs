---
title: "Units and fiat"
description: "Show every amount in Gerfaut in BTC or in sats, and add its fiat value in one of 30 currencies, with the price from mempool.space or CoinGecko."
---

Pick one unit for every amount, and add its fiat value if you want it. Open **Settings** > **General** and find the `Display` card.

## Switch between BTC and sats

Under `Unit`, select `BTC` (eight decimals, the default) or `sats`.

## Show the fiat value

<Warning>
The price source sees your IP address with each price request, once a minute while Gerfaut is open and in the background for the Android `Bitcoin price` widget. The request carries no wallet data. With a `.onion` backend, it goes [through Tor](/settings/tor#route-other-requests-through-tor) instead.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the Fiat value switch">
    Amounts gain a second line with their fiat value, except the inputs, the outputs and the fee of a transaction. The fiat value is off by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the currency">
    Under `Currency`, the seven currencies of `Every source` work with both price sources, EUR by default. The 23 others, under `CoinGecko only`, switch the source to CoinGecko with a line that says why, such as `CoinGecko is the only source that quotes INR.` Picking one of the seven again keeps CoinGecko until you select `mempool.space`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the price source">
    Under `Price source`, keep `mempool.space`, the default, or select `CoinGecko`. With [`Automatic`](/settings/backends-and-your-own-node#use-the-public-servers), mempool.space is already the first server Gerfaut asks for syncs on mainnet. The price then shows your IP address to no one new.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the rate">
    A line reads `1 BTC =` with the rate and the time of the last update. When the source does not answer, amounts show without fiat until it does.
  </Step>
</Steps>

With CoinGecko as the source, `Powered by CoinGecko` shows once on each screen with its data. It sits under the `1 BTC =` line, the price on the desktop Overview and the fiat amounts, and on the Android `Bitcoin price` widget. With mempool.space, nothing shows.

A wallet on a test network, such as signet or testnet4, shows a fiat value of 0, because test coins are worth nothing.
