---
title: "Units and fiat"
description: "Show every amount in Gerfaut in BTC or in sats, and add its fiat value in one of 30 currencies from CoinGecko, Kraken or mempool.space."
---

Pick one unit for every amount, and add a fiat value under each one if you want it. Open **Settings** > **General** and find the `Display` card.

## Switch between BTC and sats

Under `Unit`, select `BTC` (eight decimals, the default) or `sats`.

## Show the fiat value

<Warning>
Each price request reaches the price source from your IP address. It carries no wallet data.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the Fiat value switch">
    Every amount gains a second line with its fiat value. The fiat value is off by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the currency">
    Under `Currency`, the seven currencies of `Every source` work with any price source. The 23 others, under `CoinGecko only`, switch the source to CoinGecko. The default is EUR.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the price source">
    Under `Price source`, select `CoinGecko` (the default), `Kraken` or `mempool.space`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the rate">
    A line reads `1 BTC =` with the rate and the time of the last update. When the source does not answer, amounts show without fiat until it does.
  </Step>
</Steps>
