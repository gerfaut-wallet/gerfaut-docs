---
title: "Units and fiat"
description: "Show every amount in Gerfaut in BTC or in sats, and add its fiat value in one of 30 currencies from CoinGecko, Kraken or mempool.space."
---

One unit applies to every amount in Gerfaut. The fiat value is off by default and appears under each amount once you turn it on.

Open **Settings** > **General** and find the `Display` card.

## Switch between BTC and sats

Under `Unit`, select `BTC` or `sats`. The default is `BTC`.

- **`BTC`:** Every amount shows eight decimals.
- **`sats`:** Every amount shows none.

## Show the fiat value

<Warning>
Each price request reaches the source you pick from your own IP address. It carries no wallet data.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the Fiat value switch">
    Every amount gains a second line with its value, and the desktop Overview shows the `Bitcoin price` card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the currency">
    Under `Currency`, the list has two groups. `Every source` holds the seven currencies every price source quotes: EUR, USD, GBP, CHF, JPY, CAD and AUD. `CoinGecko only` holds 23 more, from INR and BRL to KRW and NZD. The default is EUR.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the price source">
    Under `Price source`, select `CoinGecko`, `Kraken` or `mempool.space`. A source that does not quote the chosen currency is greyed out, and a currency from the `CoinGecko only` group switches the source to CoinGecko on its own. The default is CoinGecko.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the rate">
    Under the controls, a line reads `1 BTC =` followed by the value in your currency and the time of the last update. When the source does not answer, amounts show without fiat until it does.
  </Step>
</Steps>
