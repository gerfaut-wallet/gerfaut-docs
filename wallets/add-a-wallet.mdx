---
title: "Add a wallet"
description: "Start watching a wallet in Gerfaut from its descriptor, extended public key or address: paste it, open a file or scan a QR code, then check the first address."
---

Give Gerfaut the public side of your wallet: the descriptor, extended public key or address your signing device or wallet software exports. Gerfaut refuses anything that contains a private key or a seed phrase.

## Add the wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the add dialog">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Desktop">
        Select the wallet switcher at the top of the sidebar, then **Add a wallet**.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Android">
        Select **Add a wallet** at the bottom of the home screen.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste, import or scan the wallet">
    Paste it in `Descriptor, extended public key, or address`, then select **Continue**. Two other ways in:

    - **Import a file:** A `.txt`, `.json`, `.desc` or `.bsms` file, such as the export of another wallet.
    - **Scan a QR code:** Plain text, UR or BBQr, animated or not. Keep the camera on an animated code until the counter reaches its total. On Android the button reads **Scan**.

    A refused input says why, under the field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the first address">
    The card reads `Recognized as` with the kind of input, then `First address`, the first address Gerfaut derives.

    <Warning>
    Compare `First address` with the first address your signing device or wallet software shows. A different address means Gerfaut watches another wallet.
    </Warning>

    For an extended public key, check the `Script type` too: see [Wallet types](/wallets/wallet-types#pick-the-script-type-of-an-extended-public-key).

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Desktop">
        <Frame caption="Gerfaut recognizes the format and derives the first address to compare before it stores anything.">
          <img src="/images/desktop/add-wallet.webp" alt="The Add a wallet dialog on desktop after pasting an extended public key: the Recognized as card with the first address, the SLIP-132 notice, the Script type list, the Name and Network fields and the Add wallet button." />
        </Frame>
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Android">
        <Frame caption="Gerfaut recognizes the pasted descriptor and shows the first address before anything is stored.">
          <img src="/images/android/add-wallet.webp" alt="The Add a wallet screen on Android after pasting a descriptor: the Recognized as card with the first address, the Name field, the network buttons and the Add wallet button." width="360" />
        </Frame>
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the wallet and select Add wallet">
    Enter a name in `Name`. The `Network` list only opens when the input fits more than one network. Gerfaut opens the wallet and runs its first sync.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Accordion title="Use a custom derivation path">
  For an extended public key used outside the usual derivation branches, open **Advanced** on the confirmation step. Enter the `Receive path`, the `Change path` and the `Key origin`, then select **Apply**. Check the new first address again.
</Accordion>

## Manage your wallets

Open **Settings** > **Wallets**. Each wallet has its own row:

- **Rename:** Enter the new name and select **Save name**. Selecting the wallet name at the top of its page does the same.
- **Icon:** Pick one of seven icons, such as `Key`, `Snowflake` or `Pin`.
- **Rescan:** Look at the wallet again from its first address, for coins received beyond the gap limit. The row then reads `Rescanned` with the number of new transactions.
- **Remove:** Stop watching the wallet once you confirm with **Remove wallet**. Nothing moves on-chain. A wallet that [Gerfaut Premium](/premium/watch-a-wallet) watches leaves the server too.
- **Reorder:** Drag a wallet by its handle. On Android, you can also hold a card on the home screen and drag it.
