Open a wallet's Policy page to see who can spend, under which condition, and when. A Miniscript descriptor with a recovery or inheritance path gets one card per path, with its timelock counted down.

## Open the Policy page

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Select **Policy** in the sidebar. The Overview also shows a digest of the policy under `Total balance`, such as `2 of 3 keys`, `Recovery in 142 days`, or `Recovery locked` until the first sync.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    On the wallet page, select **More** (the vertical dots in the app bar), then **Policy**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Read the spending paths

The page opens on a plain statement of the policy, such as `2 of 3 keys sign.` A multisig or Miniscript wallet then gets one card per spending path:

- **Path:** `Primary`, `Recovery`, `Emergency` or `Other`. The cards come in that order, the nearest lock first within each. A second primary path reads `Primary B`, and a third timed path `Recovery 3`.
- **Condition:** The signing keys and locks the path needs, such as `Any 2 of Key A, Key B, Key C`. Each signing key is a pill with its fingerprint.
- **Locks:** Each timelock in blocks and in estimated time, such as `52 560 blocks after the coin arrives ≈ 1 year`.
- **State:** `Spendable now`, a countdown such as `In 1 432 blocks ≈ 10 days`, `No coins yet`, `Needs a secret`, or `Locked` until the first sync. It turns amber when less than 30 days remain.
- **Coins:** A relative lock counts per coin, even a single one, such as `0 of 1 coin unlocked · next in 12 days`. `Next coin` and a progress bar show how long the first coin to unlock still waits. Until the first sync, the count ends in `next known after the first sync`.

<Note>
Estimated durations assume one block every 10 minutes and carry `≈` or `about`. A lock set in time follows the device clock. The chain can lag that clock by up to two hours.
</Note>

## Check the signing keys and the descriptor

The `Keys` section lists each signing key with its label, fingerprint, origin path and extended public key.

Open `Descriptor` to copy the descriptor, written as one `/<0;1>/*` descriptor when the wallet has a receive and a change branch. Gerfaut clears it from the clipboard after one minute. Its policy is written out below, such as `or(pk(Key A),and(pk(Key B),older(52560)))`.
