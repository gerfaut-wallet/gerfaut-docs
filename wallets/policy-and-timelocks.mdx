---
title: "Policy and timelocks"
description: "Read the Policy page of a wallet in Gerfaut to see every spending path of its descriptor, which keys each path needs and how long its timelock still runs."
---

The Policy page reads a wallet's descriptor as spending paths: who can spend, under which condition, and when. A single-key wallet gets one line; a Miniscript descriptor with a recovery or inheritance path gets one card per path, with its timelock counted down.

## Open the Policy page

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Select **Policy** in the sidebar. The third link under `Total balance` on the Overview opens the same page and carries a digest of the policy, such as `2 of 3 keys` or `Recovery in 142 days`.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    On the wallet page, select **More** (the vertical dots in the app bar), then **Policy**. The digest of the policy sits under the entry.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Read the policy

The page opens on one sentence that states the policy, such as `One key signs. Any coin is spendable now.` for a single-sig wallet, `2 of 3 keys sign.` for a multisig, or `Key A signs. A recovery key can spend once a coin has waited about 1 year.` for a Miniscript descriptor.

Below it, one card per spending path, the primary path first and the timed paths by nearest lock:

- **The path label:** `Primary`, `Recovery`, `Emergency`, or a numbered variant when a role appears twice.
- **The condition:** The keys and locks the path needs, such as `Any 2 of Key A, Key B, Key C` or `Key B and a wait of 52 560 blocks` (`Key B and a coin having waited 52 560 blocks` on Android), with each key as a pill carrying its fingerprint.
- **The locks:** One line per timelock, in blocks and in estimated time: `52 560 blocks after the coin arrives ≈ 1 year`, or `Block 900 000 ≈ in 4 months`. A lock the path does not depend on is marked `(optional)`.
- **The state pill:** `Spendable now`, `In 1 432 blocks ≈ 10 days`, `No coins yet`, `Needs a secret` for a path that requires a preimage, or `Locked` when nothing more is known. A relative lock is counted per coin: `3 of 7 coins unlocked · next in 12 days`, then `7 of 7 coins unlocked` once every coin is past it, where Android reads `Spendable now`.
- **The countdown:** For a waiting path, the next coin to unlock, its estimated date, and a progress bar of the wait done. The pill turns amber under 30 days.

<Note>
Estimated durations assume one block every 10 minutes and are always marked `≈`. A lock expressed in time is compared with the device clock, and the chain can lag by up to two hours. The page says so under the cards whenever such a lock exists.
</Note>

## Check the keys and the descriptor

The `Keys` section at the bottom lists each key with its label, its fingerprint, its origin path and a shortened form of the key. Under it, the `Descriptor` disclosure shows the descriptor as you imported it, with a copy button, and its normalized policy with the keys named, such as `or(pk(Key A),and(pk(Key B),older(52560)))`. On desktop that second block is labelled `Normalized policy`; on Android it is labelled `Read as`.

## Get warned before a path opens

[Gerfaut Premium](/premium/overview) sends an alert 30 days, 7 days and 1 day before a timed path becomes spendable, and again the day it opens.
