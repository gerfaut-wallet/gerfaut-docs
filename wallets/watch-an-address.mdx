---
title: "Watch an address"
description: "Watch a single bitcoin address in Gerfaut when you have no descriptor or extended public key, and see what an address alone cannot show."
---

Watch a single address to follow the coins on it, with its balance, history and UTXOs. An address alone gives no next address and no spending policy.

## Watch the address

<Steps>
  <Step title="Paste the address">
    Follow [Add a wallet](/wallets/add-a-wallet) and paste the address instead of a descriptor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the card">
    The card reads `Recognized as` followed by `Single address`, with the address under it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and select Add wallet">
    Gerfaut opens the wallet and runs its first sync.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Give the wallet the `Pin` icon in **Settings** > **Wallets**, so your list tells an address from a descriptor wallet at a glance.
</Tip>

## Know what an address cannot do

- **Receive:** The Receive page shows the one address under `Watched address`, with no next address to hand out.
- **Policy:** The Policy page reads `An address has no policy Gerfaut can read.`
- **Electrum servers:** An Electrum server cannot serve a single address. Keep the `Public API` backend on `Automatic` or on an Esplora server, or use your own Esplora. See [Backend](/settings/backends-and-your-own-node).

## Load a long history

A very active address loads its history in rounds of 1,000 transactions, while its balance already covers all of it. When more remain, select **Load older transactions** at the end of the list.

<Note>
The CSV export only holds the transactions loaded so far. Load every round before you export a complete file.
</Note>
